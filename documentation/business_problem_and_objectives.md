# Business Problem and Objectives

## 1. Business Background

Credit card companies provide credit facilities to customers based on their financial profiles and repayment behaviour. However, some customers fail to make their payments on time, which increases credit risk for financial institutions.

Understanding customer repayment patterns and identifying factors associated with credit default can help financial institutions improve their risk-monitoring processes.

## 2. Business Problem

The main business problem is to understand why some credit card customers are more likely to default on their payments than others.

The project analyses customer information, credit limits, repayment status, bill amounts and payment amounts to identify patterns associated with default.

## 3. Project Objectives

1. Analyse historical credit card customer data.
2. Understand customer repayment behaviour.
3. Identify customer segments with higher default rates.
4. Examine credit utilisation patterns.
5. Analyse payment and bill amounts.
6. Identify important factors associated with credit default.
7. Provide data-driven business recommendations.

## 4. Expected Business Value

- Better understanding of customer credit-risk patterns.
- Identification of customer groups requiring closer monitoring.
- Improved understanding of repayment behaviour.
- Support for data-driven credit-risk assessment.
